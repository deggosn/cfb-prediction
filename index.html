Bankroll

Predictions blend an opponent-adjusted efficiency rating (built from offensive/defensive EPA per play) with the current betting market line, weighted 50/50. The total (over/under) estimate is blended with the market the same way, using each team's own scoring average as the model input. Early-season weeks blend in the previous season's final rating to reduce small-sample noise. Betting suggestions are rules-based on the size of the disagreement between model and market and default to "pass" when the edge is small or data is thin — this reflects that backtesting shows the model performs close to, not clearly better than, the market (that backtest covers the spread specifically; the total's underlying scoring-average method is newer and less validated). This is a research and entertainment tool, not licensed betting or financial advice, and it does not account for injuries, weather, or breaking news beyond what's noted.
